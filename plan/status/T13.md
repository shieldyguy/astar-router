## T13.7 `astar check` (session 2): done for scene 1.1, ready for scene 2

`astar check SCENE PLAN [--max N] [-q]`: exit 0 clean, 1 findings (one a line), 2 unreadable input. `src/check/`
(`mod.rs` the check, `dist.rs` exact distances, `rules.rs` the scene's rules read on its own, `plan.rs` the plan
reader); it uses `geom`, the scene reader and the JSON reader and nothing else of the router.

- **Joined**: two pieces of a net are joined when their copper overlaps by a nanometre or more on a shared layer
  (KiCad's own rule in this build, `ItemsTouchOnLayer`: shapes, not anchors). A plane (the scene's, the plan's) is
  one piece that reaches its net's through pads, vias, and surface pads and tracks on its layer, inside its
  outline when the plan gives one. It does not fill zones: what a refilled zone really reaches stays KiCad's.
  `open-unsaid`: a net in pieces that the plan does not list in `left_out`; `open`: in pieces and the plan says so.
- **Clear**: every piece of the plan's copper against other nets' copper (the plan's, pads, tracks and vias
  already on the board), holes (bare ones; a via's or a pad's against other nets; hole to hole when the scene has
  the rule), the edge, keep-outs by what they forbid, and the outside of the outline. Kinds: short, clearance,
  hole, hole-to-hole, edge, outside, keepout, plan. Arcs as chords within 0.1 um; a shortfall under 0.5 um passes
  (KiCad's own slack); the edge within 5 um (the scene reader's chords).
- Rules as it reads docs/scene.md (T2 should compare, it shares nothing with the router's): a rule for a layer
  replaces all; else a net's own clearance over its class's; else `class_pairs` when it names the two classes;
  else the larger of the two classes'; a pad's own clearance on top (the larger wins).
- Measured: the bench five clean (28, 42, 47, 118, 243 nets joined), 0.02 to 0.10 s a board; KiCad agrees (0 new
  DRC). The three planted errors of judge_plants caught (open-unsaid AUX_L; short; clearance). On the planted
  clearance it is stricter than KiCad: the planted stub also lies on a via of a third net, which KiCad does not
  report because it renames a dangling track to the net of what it touches (so KiCad cannot see a short made by a
  piece of copper that reaches no pad of its own net). `tests/check.rs`: 10 tests on a hand-checkable board.
- In the corpus runner every row carries `check` (findings by kind, the nets only one judge finds open).
- In the fuzzer (`tests/fuzz_scene.py`, the fifth property). 750 scenes (seed 1: 150, seeds 2 and 3: 300 each;
  69, 203, 429 s), binaries at c636a09 and 458c0ff: **0 nets called joined that are not**; 0 panics; 5 scenes
  refused (every layer a plane); **127 scenes where the plan's copper breaks the scene's rules, three router
  faults** (each with a scene in tests/fuzz_found/ and a kept plan in tests/fixtures/check/ that `tests/check.rs`
